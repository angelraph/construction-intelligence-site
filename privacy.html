Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This policy covers the "Construction Intelligence Uploader" application, a piece of software built and operated by the person who runs the Construction Intelligence YouTube channel. It explains what the application does with your Google data when you sign in to authorize it.

Who this application is for

This application is used by a single channel owner to publish videos to the Construction Intelligence YouTube channel and read that channel's own performance data. It is not a public tool, and it does not sign in or manage YouTube accounts on behalf of anyone other than the channel owner.

What data we request and why

When you authorize this application with your Google account, it requests two permissions (scopes):

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload) — used to upload video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and tags to the Construction Intelligence channel.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content (yt-analytics.readonly) — used to read view counts, watch time, and similar performance metrics for videos already published on the channel, so future videos can be planned more effectively.

The application does not request access to your email, personal profile information, contacts, files, or any other Google account data beyond what is listed above.

How we store and use this data

An OAuth access token and refresh token are stored locally on the channel owner's own computer, used only to make authorized calls to the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API on the channel owner's behalf. Analytics figures retrieved from YouTube are stored locally to help plan future content. No data obtained through these permissions is sold, shared with advertisers, or provided to any third party.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens and locally stored analytics data are kept only as long as the application is in active use. Authorization can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page, which immediately stops the application from accessing your data.
